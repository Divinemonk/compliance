# Summary of Fintech Cybersecurity Laws for UPI Companies

## Key Laws and Regulations

- **IT Act, 2000**: Mandates reasonable security practices for data protection.
- **Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007**: Regulates payment systems under RBI oversight.
- **RBI Guidelines**: Requires cybersecurity frameworks, multi-factor authentication (MFA), and encryption.
- **NPCI Guidelines**: Enforces 2FA, encryption, and secure channels for UPI transactions.
- **DPDP Act, 2023**: Governs personal data protection in India.
- **AML/KYC Regulations**: Ensures identity verification and ongoing transaction monitoring.

---

## Purpose

- Protect sensitive financial and personal data.
- Ensure secure, fraud-free digital transactions.
- Maintain public trust in UPI and other digital payment systems.

---

## Compliance Tips

- Conduct regular security audits and risk assessments.
- Implement encryption, MFA, and secure communication protocols.
- Train employees on security awareness and establish an incident response plan.

---

## Challenges

- Adapting to rapidly evolving cyber threats.
- Navigating cross-border data protection and compliance regulations.
- Keeping up with new and evolving laws like the **DPDP Act, 2023**.
